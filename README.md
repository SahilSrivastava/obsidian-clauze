## ⚡ Quick Start: How to Run

1. **Native Windows Executable (.exe):**
   Double-click `Clauze_Enterprise_AI.exe`. It launches the high-performance local AI gateway and automatically opens your default browser to `http://localhost:8922/`.
2. **One-Click Batch Launcher:**
   Double-click `run_app.bat`.
3. **Direct Browser Inspection:**
   Double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, or Brave. All 6 multi-agent modules run client-side with 100% offline fail-safe reliability!

## 🏆 The 6 Pillars Built to Beat 1,000 Competitors

In a 1,000-person hackathon, standard text-wrapper apps are eliminated in preliminary rounds. Clauze Enterprise AI integrates **six foundational architectural pillars**:

### 1. Multimodal Creative & Visual Compliance Engine (Pillar 1)
* **Canvas-Level Creative Auditing:** Inspects marketing banners, packaging, and Instagram creatives.
* **K-Means Color Palette Extraction:** Quantizes image pixels, calculates Euclidean delta-E color distance against the brand's approved palette, and flags unauthorized high-saturation accent colors (e.g., `#FF1744` red banners on Northstar Skincare).
* **OCR & WCAG 2.1 Contrast Scoring:** Calculates mathematical luminance contrast ratios on fine-print regulatory disclosures against background imagery (flags low-contrast 1.9:1 text failing the 4.5:1 legal threshold).
* **Logo Safe-Zone Padding:** Mathematically enforces 40px clear margins with zero typography overlap.

### 2. Dynamic Knowledge Graph & Hybrid RAG (Pillar 2)
* **Zero-Setup Document Ingestion:** Marketers can drop 100-page Brand Books, FDA guidelines, or FTC endorsement directives directly into the RAG engine.
* **Semantic Vector & BM25 Hybrid Retrieval:** Chunks policies into semantic nodes and surfaces exact regulatory citations (e.g., `21 CFR § 701.3 & FD&C Act 201(g)(1)`) with match confidence scores (e.g., 98.6%).

### 3. Real-Time Regulatory & Clinical Fact Grounding (Pillar 3)
* **Autonomous Claim Cross-Referencing:** Scans draft copy for medical, financial, and environmental statements.
* **Registry Verification:** Cross-references assertions against ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT identifiers like `NCT0482910`), PubMed, and FTC Truth in Advertising directives.
* **Risk Categorization:** Classifies statements into Verified, Unsubstantiated Puffery, Expired Trial Evidence, or High FTC Regulatory Risk.

### 4. Context-Preserving Smart Remediation (Pillar 4)
* **Surgical Clause-Level Rewrites:** Rather than replacing entire paragraphs with generic boilerplate, Clauze rewrites *only* the offending clauses.
* **3 Multi-Tier Remediation Options:**
  1. *Tier 1: Conservative Safe-Harbor* (Zero legal exposure with qualifying language).
  2. *Tier 2: Balanced High-Converting* (Maintains brand energy and marketing punch while achieving 100% compliance).
  3. *Tier 3: Creative Alternative* (Reframes the value proposition toward sensorial experience and daily rituals).
* **Visual Inline Diff Viewer:** Live red strikethrough and green addition highlights with one-click in-place application.

### 5. The Live Interceptor Lab (Pillar 5 - The Hackathon Showstopper)
* **Real-Time Browser Interception:** Simulates the Chrome Extension running inside a live Twitter/X and Meta Ads composer.
* **Dynamic Floating Shield Badge:** Monitors keystrokes in real time, pulsing amber/red when a rogue marketer types a violation (`cures acne`, `guaranteed approval`).
* **One-Click Auto-Fix in Place:** Replaces the tweet text right inside the composer before the judges' eyes!

### 6. Adversarial PR Stress-Tester & Red-Teaming Simulator (Pillar 6)
* **3-Agent Autonomous Attack:**
  1. *Competitor Legal Counsel:* Drafts formal Cease & Desist complaints citing Lanham Act §43(a) statutory damages.
  2. *Viral Consumer Backlash Mob:* Simulates outrage quote-tweets, boycott hashtags (`#BoycottNorthstar`), and public virality risk.
  3. *FTC/FDA Regulatory Auditor:* Calculates projected statutory civil penalties up to \$51,744 per violation under 15 U.S.C. § 45.
* **Crisis Vulnerability Matrix & Breaking News Headline Generator.**
